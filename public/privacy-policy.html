Privacy policy

We keep the Hallard site useful without treating your attention as a product. Optional analytics are off until you choose to accept them.

Your choice

When you first visit Hallard, you can accept or decline optional analytics. Declining does not limit the website. You can reopen Privacy choices from the footer at any time.

What we collect

  • Information you deliberately send through your own email client when requesting a workspace.
  • Limited usage signals only after you accept analytics, such as pages viewed and anonymous interaction events.

We do not send brief text, form entries, or payment-card details to analytics tools.

Optional analytics

If you accept, Hallard may use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand how the site is used. These services receive data under their respective privacy practices. If you decline, neither service is loaded by Hallard.

Payments

Paid plans open a secure Stripe checkout. Card details are entered with Stripe, not Hallard, and are not stored by this website.

Questions or requests

For privacy questions or a request about your information, contact support@mentek.ai.
