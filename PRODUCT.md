## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + React marketing site. The approved static proof-of-concept is retained in `docs/poc` for reference.

## Users

Hallard serves two connected audiences:

- Ambitious founders, solopreneurs, and early-stage companies that need immediate capacity and want to move before competitors do.
- Operators and business leaders at growing companies who can fund a serious AI rollout and need coordinated work across teams.

Both audiences want an understandable first use case, evidence that the system will stay under human control, and a clear path to expand adoption.

## Product Purpose

Hallard is a voice-first AI workspace in which people and specialist AI agents coordinate real business work. A person can state an outcome in natural language, bring the right specialists into a live conversation, approve a plan, and follow execution through to results.

## Positioning

Hallard is not a single assistant or a narrow automation: it brings a visible, voice-coordinated AI workforce into one workspace, where specialists can discuss a job together, carry it out, and remain accountable to a human owner.

## Operating Context

Hallard is evaluated by businesses under pressure to adopt AI without creating uncontrolled, disconnected tools. The product connects conversations, meetings, tasks, calendar, files, business information, and work across administration, customer engagement, operations, HR, reporting, technology, and creative production.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Voice is the primary way work begins.
- Multiple specialist agents can participate in a live conversation, contribute expertise, coordinate responsibilities, and report progress.
- People direct work, approve plans, and review important decisions and results.
- Plans include Starter at $47/month, Business at $197/month, Business Plus at $297/month, and Enterprise with custom pricing. Final Starter entitlements remain to be defined before publication.
- English is the current website language; Spanish localization will follow separately.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the core message: “Let AI agents do the work.”
- Make the fear of falling behind or being outpaced by competitors an immediate but credible business tension.
- Present Hallard as powerful, assured, and execution-focused—not as a generic chatbot or a collection of unrelated agencies.
- The site needs purposeful motion and a high-craft technical point of view.

## Product Principles

1. Begin with the business outcome rather than a prompt.
2. Let specialists collaborate, but keep people in command.
3. Make work, ownership, and progress visible.
4. Start with one real job and expand from demonstrated value.
5. Make AI adoption feel like a business advantage, not a leap of faith.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The marketing surface should support keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, semantic page structure, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion preferences.
