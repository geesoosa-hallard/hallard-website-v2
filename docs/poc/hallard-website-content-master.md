# Hallard — Website Content Master

## 1. Company and product positioning

**Product:** Hallard  
**Company:** Mentek, LLC

**Core positioning:** Let AI agents do the work.

**Product definition:** Hallard is a voice-first AI workspace where people and AI agents work together. Users speak naturally with Hallard, bring specialist agents into live voice conversations, coordinate work across departments, and manage the execution and results in one connected workspace.

**Central product ideas:**
- Voice-first interaction: speaking is the natural starting point.
- Multi-agent voice: bring multiple specialists into the same live conversation. They can participate, collaborate, discuss a task and coordinate their work.
- Agents as workforce members: agents have identities, roles, skills, responsibilities, assignments and objectives.
- One connected workspace: conversations, meetings, calendar, tasks, files, business information and production workflows belong together.
- Human direction and oversight: people set goals, provide context, approve plans and review important decisions and results.
- Execution, not just answers: agents organize and carry out work, coordinate with one another and report progress.
- Business-wide capabilities: Hallard supports the work of an organization, from administration and customer engagement to operations, HR, reporting and creative production.

Hallard identifies the specialists needed for the job. Bring one agent into the conversation or assemble multiple specialists for a live voice discussion.

Review what the team proposes. Change anything that needs changing.

The specialists take on their assignments and work together. Follow the progress, see what needs attention and review the results.

1. Preserve the headline **Let AI agents do the work.**
2. Preserve the animated hero and its existing visual concept.
3. Lead with voice-first interaction. Do not describe voice as merely an optional input method.
4. Make multi-agent voice collaboration a central differentiator throughout the homepage, Agents page and How it works page.

11. Use examples of complete work processes and outcomes, not only lists of deliverables.
